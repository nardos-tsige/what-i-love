Our Story

Born from passion. Built on the court.

From 12 Kids to a Family

Founded in 2018 in the heart of Addis Ababa, Thunder Hoops began with just 12 young boys who shared one dream: to play basketball with pride and excellence. Today, we have grown into one of Ethiopia’s most respected basketball clubs, training over 180 athletes across youth, junior, and senior teams.

We believe basketball is more than a sport — it’s a tool for discipline, leadership, teamwork, and personal growth. Our mission is to develop not only great players, but great human beings.

Our Core Values

  • Discipline & Hard Work
  • Teamwork Above All
  • Respect for the Game
  • Excellence in Every Bounce
  • Community & Brotherhood

How We Train Our Athletes

  1. Daily skill development drills
  2. Strength & conditioning sessions
  3. Team strategy and game film study
  4. Mental toughness and leadership training
  5. Community service and character building
